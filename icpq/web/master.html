Research engine

Workers · search · AI · measured, not estimated
How the engine works, and how to use this dashboard
1 · Research (workers)
Background workers research each company in your list: they crawl the company's own website (home page, linked pages, sitemap and policy pages chosen by your ICP profile) and, if you enabled a search provider, run the profile's searches. Every page is cached, so requalifying never re-downloads anything.
2 · Qualification (your rules)
Your ICP profile defines the signals, which are mandatory, how they combine (AND / OR), what disqualifies an account and what evidence counts. Evidence must be quoted from a real page. Passages near warning words, blog/news pages, unfilled templates and vendor copy are rejected. Missing evidence is never counted as verified.
3 · Priority (A/B/C)
A qualified account gets A, B or C from your profile's priority rules (scale, fit score, key signals). Very large companies can be parked in a hold if your profile says so. The fit score explains every point it gives.
4 · AI (optional)
If you connect Claude Code, Codex or an API in Settings and turn AI on in the profile, it reviews accounts left in REVIEW. Code checks every quote word for word against the page. AI output is labelled UNVERIFIED and never changes a verdict.
5 · Evidence audit + outreach
An audit re-opens the cited page for each qualified account and confirms the quote is there (PASS / NEEDS REVIEW / FAIL). Ready for outreach = qualified A/B/C + fit verified + evidence on its own domain + audit PASS + not flagged, deduplicated across lists.
Using it
Click a Pipeline card to filter by status, or a segment card to filter by segment. Filters covers list, A/B/C, fit, use case, technology, competitor, triggers and channel. Click any row to see its evidence, source URL, reasons and history, and to edit the verdict. Export downloads exactly what you're viewing. Final files has the standard files plus a checksum manifest. The data refreshes every 30 s.
What the blocked counts mean
Retry · search blocked = the company's own site had no proof yet and a needed search couldn't run (no provider, provider paused or capped). These accounts stay queued for a retry run. No website = no verified company site was found. Neither of these counts as research complete, and neither is ever counted as a disqualification.
